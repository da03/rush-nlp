About this guide

This guide is curated by Yuntian Deng, a coauthor of WildChat and WildVis. It connects the original contributions, dataset releases, tools, and selected research using WildChat.

What the guide covers

The bibliography covers selected work on WildChat and WildVis: dataset collection, conversation analysis, exploration tools, evaluation, and training. Each entry describes the data used and the paper’s findings, with links to the paper and related resources.

Explore WildChat and LMSYS-Chat-1M with WildVisualizer, or download WildChat from the Hugging Face repositories.
