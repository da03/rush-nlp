For readers and research tools

Guide data and citations

The same annotations and source links, in reusable formats.

These files describe the research guide. Conversation data is distributed through the original dataset repositories.

Downloads

papers.json
Paper metadata, research categories, annotations, dataset relationships, and supporting sources.
papers.bib
BibTeX entries for the selected publications.
papers.csl.json
CSL-JSON references for citation managers and bibliography tools.
reading-list.md
The annotated research list in Markdown.
releases.json
Current release counts, access information, documented updates, and usage guidance.
navigation.json
Page descriptions and links for navigating this hub.

Citing the work

Cite the original WildChat paper when using the dataset, and the WildVis paper when using the visualizer. Cite downstream papers for their own methods and findings. The research list provides individual citation downloads as well as the combined files above.

Citation types, venues, and years describe the selected publication of each paper. Separately recorded arXiv dates identify preprint versions; they are not automatically dataset-release dates. Dataset names and reported counts also refer to particular releases or study snapshots.

For reproducibility, record the dataset repository, revision, access date, collection window, and any filters applied.
