Seven guides · Original papers & current connections

01

LLM decision trees for text classification without fine-tuning

Learned programs and prompting

Tree Prompting (EMNLP 2023) learns a text classifier whose internal nodes prompt a frozen language model and use its answers to select branches.

↗
02

Energy-based text generation: whole-sequence scoring, resampling and diffusion

Global sequence models

Residual Energy-Based Models for Text Generation (Yuntian Deng et al., ICLR 2020): globally normalized text generation, sample-and-score inference, and the connection to energy-based diffusion language models.

↗
03

CRFs for text generation: higher-order decoding, diffusion and candidate lattices

Joint models for parallel generation

Yuntian Deng and Alexander M. Rush’s Cascaded Text Generation (NeurIPS 2020) and Sequence-to-Lattice (EMNLP Findings 2021): generate output tokens with structured inference and connect their dependencies to joint diffusion decoding.

↗
04

Attention as a Latent Variable: Modeling a Distribution over Attention

Probabilistic attention and latent inference

Yuntian Deng and colleagues’ Latent Alignment and Variational Attention (NeurIPS 2018) models attention as a latent variable with output marginalization and learned posterior inference.

↗
05

Evaluating generated text beyond fluency: story structure and entity consistency

Coherence, coreference and narrative evaluation

Model Criticism for Long-Form Text Generation (Deng, Kuleshov and Rush, EMNLP 2022) evaluates discourse coherence, coreference and topicality through latent structure.

↗
06

Diffusion rendering: LaTeX formulas and SMILES molecule images

Markup-to-image diffusion · ICLR 2023

Learned diffusion rendering turns supplied LaTeX code into formula images and SMILES strings into molecular drawings. Original ICLR 2023 work by Yuntian Deng, Noriyuki Kojima, and Alexander M. Rush.

↗
07

Interactive Training: human and LLM control of ongoing model training

Live intervention · EMNLP 2025

Humans and LLM agents use live feedback to change learning rates and data or request checkpoints in an active job. Interactive Training introduced the framework at EMNLP 2025.
