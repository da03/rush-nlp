About

A collection of methods
and their connections.

These notes explain research on language models and structured learning, from original formulations to current applications.

How to use the collection

Start with a guide, work through its example, and use the method comparisons to choose the relevant papers. Each guide links to original sources and a focused reading list. Examples labeled illustrative use fixed inputs and probabilities to explain an idea.

Sources and authorship

The collection is written by Yuntian Deng, an author of the original papers highlighted in these guides. All authors are listed on each paper record. Related work is included for its methodological connection, with the differences explained in the guide.

Conference publication years and the dates of first arXiv postings are separate fields. The bibliography follows the inspected primary papers and publication records. It is a focused reading collection, rather than a complete survey of every field.

Reusable formats

Readable pages, citations, and machine-readable records are generated from one corpus. Download the bibliography and guide contents to use them in a reading workflow.
