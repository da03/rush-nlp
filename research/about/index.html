About

A collection of methods
and their connections.

These notes explain research on language models and structured learning, from original formulations to current applications.

How to use the collection

Start with a guide, try its interactive example, and compare the methods. Follow the paper links for derivations, experiments, and implementations.

Sources and authorship

The collection is written by Yuntian Deng, an author of the original papers highlighted in these guides. Each paper record lists all authors. The guides compare the original methods with research on diffusion language models, agent decision trees, and training control.

Paper records list publication years and dates of first public versions so you can trace when a method was introduced and cite its published version.

Reusable formats

Use BibTeX or CSL JSON with a reference manager, or read and annotate the Markdown guides. Download the bibliography and guide contents.
